# STE: Simplified Technical Russian (УТР) — 2026-11-15

## Conclusion

Use selected УТР techniques inside Russian clarity mode, not as Russian
ASD-STE100 or a third operating mode. Most of the method was already present.
The final candidate and baseline both pass 21/21 checks. This is compatibility
evidence on a small, targeted sample, not evidence of superior writing quality.

## Sources and applicability

Sources were read on 2026-11-15; the run finished after local midnight.

- Владислав Москалёнок, [«Лаконичность без примитивизма»](https://protext.su/pro/lakonichnost-bez-primitivizma/),
  2015-07-20. Describes Bercut's organization-specific conventions and editorial
  process. Its [published table](https://protext.su/pro/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/UTR.png)
  was read as an image, also supplied by the user. The PDF extractor failed;
  no claim here depends on extracting the PDF.
- Инна Якименко, [«Как писать кратко и понятно»](https://protext.su/pro/kak-pisat-kratko-i-ponyatno/),
  2014-01-10. Explicitly presents a practitioner's working version of rules,
  not a normative УТР specification.
- ГОСТ Р 58049—2017: [official Росстандарт catalogue](https://protect.gost.ru/gost/details/4183a5cf-a3af-4e95-97bd-c7bb4bb27063)
  establishes the title, scope, order 2129-ст dated 2017-12-29, and introduction
  date 2018-06-01. The [text via ГАРАНТ](https://base.garant.ru/73850744/)
  was read directly, including sections 8.1 and 8.2.

The ГОСТ primarily governs written translation of aviation operational
technical documentation. Section 1 also permits its use for other documentation.
It is not automatically a universal obligation for Russian prompts or UI.
Sections 8.1.3–8.1.8 address approved project terminology resources. Sections
8.2.5.1 and 8.2.6.1 recommend a maximum of 20 words for instructions and
descriptions, with special counting rules in 8.2.5.1. This is not English
STE's 20/25 split. A selected-rules review is not whole-standard verification.

## Adopted and rejected

Adopted in `skills/ste/references/languages.md`:

- Direct word order when it clarifies responsibility; conditions remain first.
- Parallel plural imperatives, numbered execution order, separate independent
  actions, and preservation of simultaneous actions.
- Shorter introductions and participial clauses only when qualifications survive.
- Approved, consistent terminology; no assumption that rights and privileges
  are interchangeable in an access model.
- Missing actions are questions for the author, not opportunities to invent
  a plausible procedure.
- The source and applicability of the ГОСТ's length recommendation.

The articles are useful but their examples are not semantic authorities:

1. The 2015 table changes «пользователю, который не имеет прав доступа» to
   «пользователю». That deletes an eligibility condition. Safe alternative:
   «пользователю без прав доступа».
2. The 2014 article changes 2–3 minutes, potentially longer depending on
   configuration, to no more than 3–4 minutes. That invents an upper bound and
   removes the configuration exception.
3. The table completes «В окне опция “Доступ к системе”» as a selection action.
   Without further context, the fragment does not establish whether to select,
   enable, disable, or inspect the option.

No DITA/CAT dependency, universal Russian word quota, or mandatory explicit
«вы» was added. Natural UI fragments and impersonal errors remain permitted.
Source provenance is in `skills/ste/README.md`; the root README is synchronized.

## Method

Existing promptfoo fixture provider, Claude Sonnet 5.5, medium effort, clean
sessions, three repeats per scenario. Seven scenarios: four existing regressions
and three new synthetic adaptations of the supplied ProText examples.
The agent receives only the fixture and task, not the checker or good result.
Assertions are code, not an LLM judge or a style score.

Baseline `main`: `104116ffa9806717a8d138f91f8da772b5c025a0`.

The STE files at these two refs are identical. Candidate: working-tree changes.

## Observed output and limitations

All nine final artifacts from the three new scenarios were manually inspected.
Typical procedural result:

```text
Администратор назначает пользователю права доступа только после
согласования заявки владельцем системы.

1. Откройте окно «Права доступа».
2. Выберите опцию «Доступ к системе».
3. Нажмите «ОК».
```

Typical fidelity result:

```text
Назначить права доступа пользователю без прав доступа.

Процедура занимает 2–3 минуты. В зависимости от конфигурации
может потребоваться больше.

Права доступа может назначить только администратор после
согласования заявки владельцем системы.
```

In all final ambiguity artifacts, the incomplete option fragment remained
unchanged and the answer flagged the missing action. Both nested and flat
numbering occurred. One explanation asserted that the fragment was an
independent action, although that structure is uncertain; passing these checks
does not make every explanation authoritative. Explanations were often much
longer than the edited text. Neither explanatory brevity nor stylistic polish
was scored.

- `npm test`: 166/166 passed, including good/bad fixtures and negative mutations
  for dropped eligibility, invented duration, missing exception, new duty,
  and invented action; also positive equivalents for active wording/nesting.
- `git diff --check`: passed.
- These are synthetic, targeted examples, not production incidents or held-out
  generalization tests. Candidate guidance includes closely related examples.
- Three repeats on one model cannot establish reliable population effects.
- Checks test observable safeguards, not full semantic equivalence or style.
- No full ГОСТ audit, terminology approval, or automated 20-word counting eval
  was performed. No formal ASD-STE100 lexical verification was performed.
